Key discoveries

FAIL 4rc-cached checkpoint is broken in eval

Trained loss converged BELOW wan22 (final total 0.015 vs 0.030, point 0.0019 vs 0.0040), yet cl=16 eval on the same training sequences returns 279.9 mm hand-vert / 296.9 mm hand-joint / 121.0 mm ADD-S — i.e. 11–13× worse than wan22 on the same data. Eval is not a noise floor: per-window errors are 250–330 mm consistently and rotation error is 70–178° random. wan22 uses the identical eval pipeline and gets ~24 mm. Both training and eval call MultiSeqPyramidCache.gather(out_dtype=fp32) on the same cache dir, so this is not the simple fp16/fp32 path mismatch from feedback_cached_pyramid_eval_gap. Needs investigation before any 4rc-based ablation is trusted.

FINDING wan22-pe shows a 14× train→OOT gap on subject-07

In-train (subject-01): hand_vert 24.2 mm, hand_joint 23.1 mm, ADD-S 8.8 mm. Out-of-train (subject-07): hand_vert 341.0 mm, hand_joint 368.1 mm, ADD-S 151.9 mm. 50-sequence single-subject training is far too narrow for cross-subject generalization. The cl=16 eval still works on subject-07 (no NaNs/explosions), so the model isn't broken — it just hasn't seen enough subject diversity.

FIX subject-07 manifest had stale tracked-start values

manifests/dexycb_subject07_test.json declared tracked_start=5 / tracked_end=69 for all 5 sequences, but DexYCB's joint_3d sentinel-check rejects frames 0..6/10/8/12/16 in those sequences. precompute_gt_cache.py and precompute_4rc_feats.py threw HandNotTrackedError on the first frame. Patched to the longest contiguous tracked window — written to manifests/dexycb_subject07_test_fixed.json (lengths 67/63/65/59/57) and used by all evals.

FINDING Mini-eval was no-op'd during cached training

p2_mini_eval.run_mini_eval shorts to NaN whenever encoder_mode='cached'. Both runs logged 'mini_eval_skipped reason=encoder=wan22_cached' every 2k steps. So the catastrophic 4rc-cached eval gap was invisible during training; only this offline cl=16 eval surfaced it.

Caveats

  • Single-subject training (50 sub-01 right-hand sequences). The OOT eval is on 5 sub-07 sequences — small N; treat the magnitudes as a directional signal, not a precise generalization measurement.
  • ADD-S uses sym-min over the YCB-V symmetry group on a 1024-pt CAD; the older logs/wan22_pe_pt_b35ydgie/seed0/eval_cl16/aggregate.json used a 256-pt FPS-only subset and reported simpler aligned ADD-S — those numbers (8.80 mm) are cross-checked here as obj_add_s_simple.
  • Each condition runs a single forward at fp32 with seed=0; no MANO post-fit. Numerical noise within the seed-0 sampling explains <0.5 mm differences across re-runs.
  • pt-swhtyb0b accidentally executed JOB_B's body because make_submission shallow-copied the template (both submissions ended up sharing roles[0]). pt-1xrqjmtn (correct Job B) re-ran the same body and overwrote pt-swhtyb0b's outputs; pt-2rv8491f delivered the missing wan22 OOT pipeline. Final aggregates are from pt-1xrqjmtn + pt-2rv8491f.
