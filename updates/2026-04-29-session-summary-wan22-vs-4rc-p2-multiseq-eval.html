Key discoveries

FIX INVEST-1 resolved: d7cbf65 inline temporal_pe trap fully explained the 4rc-cached 70× train/eval gap

Commit d7cbf65 (Apr 28 11:55 UTC) added an inline additive sinusoidal temporal PE on queries inside HandDecoder / ObjectPoseDecoder / TemporalSelfAttnBlock, defaulting to temporal_pe=True. The PE is computed inline from clip-position 0..15 and the flag is a plain Python bool — neither parameter nor persistent buffer. Loading state_dict reports miss=0 unexpected=0 even though the saved weights were trained against a different setting. The 4rc-cached training process started at 07:36 UTC (before the 11:55 commit) and ran with PE=False; final.pt was saved at 20:47 with the trained weights. Every subsequent offline eval (eval_with_predictions.py, repro_4rc_eval.py) loaded the ckpt into the post-d7cbf65 head and silently got temporal_pe=True, injecting a PE term the weights never saw. Effect: training-style replay drops from 0.140 m L1 (broken) to 0.0021 m L1 (matches trainer-logged 0.00193 within 10%). Chunk-aligned single-clip drops 137 mm → 29 mm. Object rotation error drops 173.7° → 0.46°. All four eval metrics (point/joint/rot/trans) match trainer log to ≤ 16% after the fix. Fix shipped as Option A: scratch/eval_with_predictions.py exposes --temporal-pe {auto,on,off}; scratch/repro_4rc_eval.py honors TEMPORAL_PE env var; helper apply_temporal_pe_setting() + temporal_pe_from_ckpt() in stream3r/heads/hoi3r_v2/temporal_pe.py. Memory: feedback_temporal_pe_inline_flag_trap.md. Durable fix (Option B, OPT-B in pending) writes arch_meta into the checkpoint to make the auto path actually auto.

FINDING wan22-pe inter-window staircase is partially mitigatable but B (Wan2.2 4× compression) is the floor

Per-frame errors within a single cl=16 window for sub-01 seq00: frame 1=33.8 mm, frame 2=54.5 mm (cold-start spike), frames 3–5 settle to ~30 mm, then drift between 23–36 mm for the remaining 12 frames. Window boundaries show 3–5 mm jumps. Overlap-averaging (stride=8 sliding window) on indices 0/25/49 gives modest improvements: hand_vert mean 23.05 → 21.96 mm (−4.7%), hand boundary_jump 4.74 → 4.08 mm (−14%), obj ADD-S 9.88 → 8.72 mm (−12%); object boundary_jump regressed +14.6% (likely SE(3)-averaging math bug). Decision rule (boundary ↓50% AND mean ↓10%) NOT met. Pattern most consistent with Wan2.2's 4× temporal compression (only 4 distinct latents per cl=16 window) being a real floor; PE breaks intra-block degeneracy but the underlying signal is still ×4 downsampled. Recommendation: do not land overlap-averaging in production driver as-is; first run stride=4 monotonicity check + hand-only overlap to disambiguate. If gains saturate → escalate to denser-grained encoder. Driver: scratch/wan22_overlap_eval.py. Output: logs/wan22_pe_pt_b35ydgie/seed0/overlap_eval/.

FINDING OOT signal: right-hand-only model evaluated against 3-out-of-5 left-hand sequences

The corrected 4rc-cached OOT mean is 561.9 mm hand_vert across 5 sequences, but disaggregating by hand_side reveals: right-hand (seq00, seq01) mean = 146.5 mm; left-hand (seq02, seq03, seq04) mean = 838.8 mm. Training manifest dexycb_subject01_right_manifest.json contains only right-hand sequences, so the left-hand evaluations are out-of-distribution by handedness, not just by subject. The right-only OOT signal (146.5 mm hand_vert, 117.6 mm hand_joint, 130.1 mm obj ADD-S) is the meaningful generalization gap: 41.8 → 146.5 mm hand_vert is a 3.5× cross-subject overfit, comparable to v1 trajectory-head's pattern. The 720/920 mm numbers in the original page were a compound of the d7cbf65 trap AND the OOD-handedness contamination.

FINDING Neither model generalizes cross-subject — but the magnitude after correction is much smaller for 4rc

Single-subject training (50 sub-01 right-hand sequences) shows train→OOT gaps for both encoders. wan22 in-train hand_vert 24 mm → OOT 341 mm (14×). 4rc-cached after PE-fix: in-train hand_vert 41.8 mm → OOT right-only 146.5 mm (3.5×). Object pose follows the same pattern — wan22 ADD-S 8.8 → 151.9 mm (17×); 4rc ADD-S 8.76 → 130.1 mm (15×). Object pose has a much larger relative cross-subject gap than hand pose for both encoders. 50-seq single-subject is too narrow regardless of encoder. INVEST-4 (multi-subject manifest) targets this directly.

FIX subject-07 manifest had stale tracked-start values

manifests/dexycb_subject07_test.json declared tracked_start=5 for all 5 sequences, but DexYCB's joint_3d sentinel-check rejects frames 0..6/10/8/12/16. precompute_gt_cache.py and precompute_4rc_feats.py raised HandNotTrackedError. Patched to the longest contiguous tracked window in manifests/dexycb_subject07_test_fixed.json (lengths 67/63/65/59/57) and used by all evals.

FINDING Mini-eval was no-op'd during cached training

p2_mini_eval.run_mini_eval shorts to NaN whenever encoder_mode='cached'. Both runs logged 'mini_eval_skipped reason=encoder=wan22_cached' every 2k steps. So the 4rc-cached cold-start failure was completely invisible during training — the FAILURE_PROTOCOL gate (kill at step 6000 if hand_joint > 6.2 mm) never fired because no eval ever ran. Re-enabling mini-eval for cached encoders is INVEST-3.

Caveats

  • The 4rc-cached numbers in this page have been UPDATED 2026-04-29 after INVEST-1. Original (broken) numbers are preserved in EVAL-3 / EVAL-4 entries with explicit 'broken — pre-fix' tags so the page tells the full story. Corrected numbers are tagged '— CORRECTED' in headline_stats and in EVAL-3-CORRECTED / EVAL-4-CORRECTED entries.
  • wan22-pe numbers (24.2 / 23.1 / 8.8 mm in-train; 341.0 / 368.1 / 151.9 mm OOT) are unaffected — that run trained AFTER d7cbf65 with temporal_pe=True. Cross-check by running --temporal-pe off on it and confirming metrics WORSEN is captured as WAN22-PE-CROSSCHECK in tasks_pending.
  • The original 'cold-start CoM monotonic decrease' analysis (327 → 207 mm across cl=16) was an artefact of the d7cbf65 PE-injection bug, not a real causal-decoder phenomenon. The corrected per-window error pattern for 4rc-cached is consistent with sequence-position effects (~52 → 39 → 23 mm across windows 0/16/32 of seq00), not a 270 mm rigid offset.
  • OOT all-5 mean is dominated by 3 left-hand sequences (838.8 mm hand_vert vs 146.5 mm right-only). Use the right-only mean for cross-subject signal interpretation until OOT-LEFT-HAND lands.
  • Single-subject training (50 sub-01 right-hand sequences). The OOT eval is on 5 sub-07 sequences — small N; treat the magnitudes as a directional signal, not a precise generalization measurement.
  • ADD-S uses sym-min over the YCB-V symmetry group on a 1024-pt CAD; the older logs/wan22_pe_pt_b35ydgie/seed0/eval_cl16/aggregate.json used a 256-pt FPS-only subset and reported simpler aligned ADD-S — those numbers (8.80 mm) are cross-checked here as obj_add_s_simple.
  • Each condition runs a single forward at fp32 with seed=0; no MANO post-fit. Numerical noise within the seed-0 sampling explains <0.5 mm differences across re-runs.
  • pt-swhtyb0b accidentally executed JOB_B's body because make_submission shallow-copied the template (both submissions ended up sharing roles[0]). pt-1xrqjmtn (correct Job B) re-ran the same body and overwrote pt-swhtyb0b's outputs; pt-2rv8491f delivered the missing wan22 OOT pipeline. Final aggregates (pre-PE-fix) were from pt-1xrqjmtn + pt-2rv8491f; corrected 4rc aggregates were re-run locally on the debug container post-INVEST-1.
