Key findings

PASS V1 wins generalization on s1_test (-63% hand, -20% obj)

On unseen subject-07 sequences (avg over 2 working evals), V1 reduces hand joint L2 from 324.1 mm to 132.3 mm and object L2 from 163.9 mm to 130.9 mm. The single-Φ trajectory framing + LoRA-adapted encoder delivers a meaningful generalization improvement that frozen-encoder + 4-head topology could not.

FAIL V1 fails the spec target of obj L2 ≤ 60 mm on s1_test

Spec §7 success criterion was obj L2 ≤ 60 mm on s1_test (≥40% reduction from 104 mm baseline). V1 reaches 130.9 mm — better than baseline (-20%) but far above the bar. Consistent with memory project_overfit_subject01 ("single-subject overfit dominates"); architectural shifts at this scale are dwarfed by the ~90 mm train/test gap.

PASS Cross-clip-length stability holds (1.65× vs spec target 2×)

V1 trained at variable T∈[2,16] and evaluated at full 63-frame clips: subject-01 hand 8.77→14.51 mm = 1.65× regress (success criterion: < 2×). Confirms that single-Φ fixes the cl=4→cl=16 collapse documented in memory project_cl_train_eval_mismatch.

FINDING Procrustes hurts subject-01 obj (+75%)

On the training sequence, V1 obj L2 (14.51 mm) is worse than baseline (8.30 mm) — the SE(3) Procrustes wrapper rigidifies object output relative to baseline's free vertex regression. Could be relaxed by lowering --obj-procrustes-weight or replacing with quotient-symmetry loss for v1.1.
