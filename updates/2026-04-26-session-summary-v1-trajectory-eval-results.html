Key discoveries

FINDING V1 wins s1_test, loses subject-01 obj — Procrustes is the variable

On unseen subject-07 sequences V1 reduces hand joint L2 by 59% (324→132 mm) and obj L2 by 20% (164→131 mm) versus the anchor. On the training sequence V1 hand joint L2 wins by 24% (16.12→12.35 mm) but V1 obj L2 loses by 75% (8.30→14.51 mm). The SE(3) Procrustes wrapper rigidifies object motion — a generalization win on unseen subjects, but it gives up the legacy free-vertex regression's ability to fit object deformation noise on the training sequence. Mitigation for v1.1: lower --obj-procrustes-weight or add quotient-symmetry loss.

FIX ACP pt-wlapue0u: training succeeded; FAILED state was a tee artifact

Training completed all 4000 optimizer steps; model_final.pt (7.8 GB) + 8 checkpoints + history.json all saved. ACP marked the job FAILED because the startup_script wrapped the launcher with `bash launcher.sh 2>&1 | tee logs/V1_trajectory_head/train.log` while logs/V1_trajectory_head/ didn't exist yet. tee returned nonzero → set -euo pipefail propagated. Fix for future submits: mkdir -p before tee, or drop tee entirely (stage2_train.py already writes history.json). Recorded in feedback_acp_tee_logdir.md.

FIX Kabsch SVD under bf16 autocast needs explicit autocast disable + fp32 cast

torch.linalg.svd and torch.det are not implemented for bfloat16 on CUDA (svd_cuda_gesvdjBatched, lu_factor_cublas). Plain `H.float()` is insufficient because autocast wraps surrounding matmul (V @ U.T) and downcasts back. Pattern: `with torch.amp.autocast(device_type='cuda', enabled=False): ... fp32 closed-form ...; cast back outside the block.` Recorded in feedback_kabsch_autocast_disable.md.

FINDING hand_l2_mm means different things across head_mode

Same metric key in legacy = vertex L2 over 256 canonical samples; in V1 trajectory = joint L2 over 21 MANO joints. Never compare directly — vertex L2 is mechanically larger. Cross-experiment table separates `Train hand-vert` and `Train hand-joint` columns. To unify, V1 needs MANO post-fit MPVPE (currently blocked by missing MANO_RIGHT.pkl).

FINDING Spec §7 partial-pass = multi-subject expansion is the next move

Spec §7 success requires both s1_test obj L2 ≤ 60 mm AND beats anchor + cl-stability < 2×. V1 passes the relative criteria but misses the absolute target by 2× (131 vs 60 mm). Per the spec's pre-registered fallback: 'failing condition (1) but passing (2) is partial — the trajectory framing helps consistency but not generalization, and the next move is multi-subject manifest expansion rather than further architectural change.' Train V1 on all 10 DexYCB subjects (~1000 seq) before any further architectural change.
