Key discoveries

FIX RGB overlay anchor bug

The old renderer always added a MANO-derived frame0 hand translation. Under the current camera0 visualization contract that extra anchor shifts the overlay away from the image hand. The refreshed overlay summaries record hand_anchor_mode=none and hand_anchor=[0,0,0].

FINDING Metric decomposition retained

The published metrics include raw MPJPE, root-aligned MPJPE, PA-MPJPE, WA-MPJPE, W-MPJPE, and gamma L2 for the same predictions.pt records.
