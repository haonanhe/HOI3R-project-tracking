Cross-experiment findings

WIN V1 is the only experiment with measured s1_test improvement

All motion-grounding ablation cells were evaluated on the subject-01 training sequence only. The frozen 3-lever anchor (pt-ivn0p7l0) ran s1_test eval but failed (~324 mm hand, ~164 mm obj). V1 is the first cell to deliver a meaningful improvement on unseen subjects: −59% hand joint L2 and −20% obj L2 against the anchor. Architectural ablations under the legacy topology never moved the test metric.

FINDING Train metrics are not directly comparable across topology families

Legacy 4-head cells report hand vertex L2 (over 256 canonical samples). V1 reports hand joint L2 (over 21 MANO joints — a sparse, well-supervised subset). Vertex L2 is mechanically larger than joint L2 because vertex distance includes palm-face spread. Apples-to-apples joint-L2: anchor 16.12 mm vs V1 12.35 mm — V1 wins by 24%. Apples-to-apples vertex-L2 will require V1 + MANO post-fit (blocked by missing MANO_RIGHT.pkl on the debug container).

FAIL Procrustes wrapper costs +75% on training obj

V1 obj L2 on subject-01 train (14.51 mm) is significantly worse than the ablation winner (4.79 mm) and the anchor (8.30 mm). The SE(3) Procrustes wrapper enforces rigid object motion — appropriate for s1_test where the model must generalize but harmful on a single sequence where the legacy free-vertex regression can fit object deformation noise. Mitigations: lower --obj-procrustes-weight, or use --obj-correspondence-weight for a softer rigidity prior.

WIN Single-Φ topology beats source/target factorization on cross-clip-length

Memory project_cl_train_eval_mismatch documented motion heads regressing 10× from cl=4 to cl=16 eval. V1 trains at variable T∈[2,16] and evaluates at full 63-frame clips with only 1.65× regress (8.77→14.51 mm obj). Single-Φ derives Δ from Φ(τ)−Φ(t), so cycle/transport identities hold by construction; legacy heads learn ΔP and Φ separately and can drift apart.

FINDING Spec §7 partial-pass: trajectory framing helps consistency, not generalization

V1 misses the spec §7 absolute target (s1_test obj L2 ≤ 60 mm; achieved 131 mm) but passes both relative criteria (beats anchor on hand and obj; cl-stability < 2×). Per spec fallback: 'failing condition (1) but passing (2) is partial — the trajectory framing helps consistency but not generalization, and the next move is multi-subject manifest expansion rather than further architectural change.' Expand training manifest beyond subject-01.

Next direction (per spec §7 partial-pass clause)

V1 shifts both metrics in the right direction but misses the absolute s1_test obj-L2 ≤ 60 mm bar by 2×. The spec's pre-registered fallback says: "the next move is multi-subject manifest expansion rather than further architectural change." Concretely:

  • Expand training manifest from subject-01 (1×100 seq) to all 10 DexYCB subjects (~1000 seq) — 10× more variety on hand pose + object grasp.
  • Re-run V1 with same architecture; expected s1_test obj L2 should drop into the 60-80 mm range.
  • If multi-subject doesn't break 60 mm, then revisit Procrustes weight tuning (currently --obj-procrustes-weight 1.0) — symmetric YCB objects may benefit from quotient-symmetry loss (deferred to v1.1 per spec §9).
