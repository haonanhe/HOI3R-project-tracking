Key discoveries

FIX INVEST-1 root cause: d7cbf65 inline temporal_pe trap

The 2026-04-29 published 4rc-cached numbers (in-train 280/297/121, OOT 718/920/171) were corrupt. Root cause: HandDecoder/ObjectPoseDecoder/TemporalSelfAttnBlock store an inline temporal-PE behind a Python bool flag (post commit d7cbf65, 2026-04-28 11:55 UTC). The flag has no representation in state_dict, so loading the pre-d7cbf65 4rc-cached checkpoint into the post-d7cbf65 head reported `missing=0 unexpected=0` and silently applied a transform the trained weights never saw. Fix: scratch/eval_with_predictions.py now exposes --temporal-pe {auto,on,off} via apply_temporal_pe_setting() and stamps `temporal_pe=False applied to 4 modules` in stdout. Re-running with PE off drops in-train hand_vert from 279.9 to 41.8 mm (6.7x), hand_joint from 296.9 to 34.3 mm (8.7x), and obj ADD-S from 121.0 to 8.76 mm (13.8x). The 4rc-cached run is no longer broken on its own train data.

FINDING OOT signal: right-hand-only model evaluated against 3-out-of-5 left-hand sequences

The corrected OOT mean is 561.9 mm hand_vert across 5 sequences, but disaggregating by hand_side reveals: right-hand (seq00, seq01) mean = 146.5 mm; left-hand (seq02, seq03, seq04) mean = 838.8 mm. Training manifest dexycb_subject01_right_manifest.json contains only right-hand sequences, so the left-hand evaluations are out-of-distribution by handedness, not just by subject. The right-only OOT signal (146.5 mm) is the meaningful generalization gap: 41.8 (in-train) -> 146.5 (OOT, same handedness) is a 3.5x gap, comparable to v1 trajectory-head's overfit pattern. Future OOT manifests for this checkpoint should filter to right-hand only or include a left-hand training sweep.

FINDING wan22-pe numbers are NOT affected by the trap

wan22-pe (pt-6pa0b3dj) trained AFTER d7cbf65 with temporal_pe=True so its 2026-04-29 published metrics (in-train 24.2/23.1/8.8, OOT 341.0/368.1/151.9) are valid. Cross-check: running scratch/eval_with_predictions.py against the wan22-pe checkpoint with --temporal-pe off would WORSEN metrics (PE was actually used in training). Wan22 numbers stand.

FINDING OOT object ADD-S still degraded after fix

Even after PE-off, OOT obj ADD-S is 123.0 mm overall (right-only 130.1 mm, left-only 117.9 mm), vs in-train 8.76 mm — a 14x gap, similar in shape to the wan22 pattern (8.8 -> 151.9). This is a real cross-subject generalization signal, not a bug: the object pose head overfits to subject-01's grasp distribution. Same conclusion as the original 2026-04-29 page on this point: 50-seq single-subject training is too narrow.

Caveats

  • wan22-pe numbers (24.2/23.1/8.8 in-train; 341.0/368.1/151.9 OOT) are unaffected — that run trained AFTER d7cbf65 with temporal_pe=True. Only the 4rc-cached checkpoint, trained 2026-04-28 07:36-20:47 UTC (started before the 11:55 UTC commit), was caught by the trap.
  • OOT mean is dominated by 3 left-hand sequences (838.8 mm hand_vert). Training was right-hand only; the right-only OOT mean (146.5 mm) is the real generalization signal.
  • Object ADD-S OOT degradation (8.76 -> 123.0 mm, ~14x) is a real cross-subject overfit signal independent of the PE trap — same pattern as the original 2026-04-29 finding, just with corrected baselines.
  • Per-window CoM monotonic-decrease symptom from the original 2026-04-29 'cold-start' analysis was an artifact of the PE bug, not a real causal-decoder phenomenon. The correct in-train per-window error pattern (e.g. 52.7 -> 39.5 -> 22.9 mm across windows 0/16/32 of seq00) is consistent with sequence-position effects, not a 270 mm rigid offset.
